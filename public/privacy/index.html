DEVKIT • PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 9, 2026

TL;DR: DevKit is designed with a strict zero-data-collection architecture. All system setting toggles, Logcat diagnostic logs, and latency tests remain 100% local on your Android device. We do not collect, track, or share your personal data.

Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the DevKit mobile application (package name: com.andikatp.devkit), developed independently by Andika. DevKit provides advanced developer options toggles, system setting controls, ADB shortcuts, logcat monitoring, and device diagnostics.

Information Collection & Processing

DevKit operates entirely locally on your device. We do not collect, aggregate, store on remote servers, or transmit any personal information to third parties.

  • System Settings & ADB Toggles: Features such as Developer Options, USB Debugging, Layout Bounds, Show Taps, Pointer Location, Stay Awake, GPU Profiling, and Font/Animation Scale read and modify local system settings via Android system APIs or local WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS permissions. None of these settings are recorded outside your device.
  • Logcat & Diagnostic Logs: Logcat log viewer streams system and application logs locally using READ_LOGS permissions. Logs are rendered exclusively in real-time in the app user interface and are never exported, logged to cloud servers, or transmitted off your device.
  • Device Diagnostics & Ping Tests: Network latency and ICMP ping tests perform diagnostic queries directly between your device and the destination host (e.g., Google DNS 8.8.8.8). No network metrics or telemetry are reported back to us.

Permissions Requested

To provide developer utility tools, DevKit requests specific system permissions. All requested permissions are used exclusively to enable the advertised features:

  • android.permission.WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS: Optional permission granted via ADB to allow direct, one-tap toggling of system developer settings without navigating system menus.
  • android.permission.READ_LOGS: Allows reading system logcat output for real-time developer debugging.
  • android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used for running diagnostic ICMP ping tests and interacting with Google Play Billing API for Pro licensing.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: Enables Google Play In-App Billing for DevKit Pro purchase verification.

In-App Purchases & Security

DevKit offers optional DevKit Pro lifetime access via Google Play In-App Purchases (IAP). All financial transactions and payment processing are handled securely directly by Google Play Store under Google's Privacy Policy.

DevKit does not store or process your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or payment credentials. Purchase status is cached securely on your device using encrypted key-value storage (FlutterSecureStorage) strictly to verify your Pro entitlement across app restarts.

Third-Party Services

The application relies solely on official Google Play Billing services for purchase management. DevKit does not integrate any third-party ad networks, telemetry trackers, or external analytics SDKs.

Children's Privacy

DevKit is an advanced utility tool intended for software developers and power users. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or DevKit, please reach out via our support channel or developer contact on Google Play Store.
